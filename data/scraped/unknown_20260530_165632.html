About Domino’s

Domino's Pizza, founded in 1960 and headquartered in Michigan, United States, is a market leader in the pizza delivery industry. It has the second-largest franchise chain in the US and worldwide, with over 10,000 franchised and corporate stores. It forayed into the Indian market in 1996, and today it has more than 54% market share and 70% share in the pizza home delivery segment. They pride themselves on using freshly cut vegetables and high-quality ingredients in making delicious pizzas, fillings, sandwiches, toppings, and so much more! Check out super deals and special discount codes, including Domino’s Wednesday offers, Freaky Friday Offers, Weekend Offers, and more.

There is no other brand that comes to mind when you say Pizza, except Domino's. They have some of the tastiest pizzas in all kinds of flavors. Domino’s Saving Tips & Tricks

  1. Enrol in the Cheesy Rewards programme via the Domino's app; every order of ₹350 or above earns 100 points, and 600 points can be redeemed for a free regular pizza.
  2. Domino's runs seasonal promotions tied to events such as the Republic Day sale, the IPL season, and major Indian festivals; check the Offers section in the app during these periods for limited-time codes and flat discounts.
  3. The Everyday Value section offers two regular pizzas from ₹99 each or two medium pizzas from ₹199 each on selected items; no coupon code is needed and the offer applies on Domino's own ordering channels only, not on aggregators.
  4. Get Domino’s app-only offers, discounts, and exclusive promo codes on select orders. First-time users on the Domino's app can receive a complimentary Choco Lava Cake (worth ₹109) on their first order, subject to store availability.
  5. Order combo meals such as Meal for 4 or Meal for 2 to save compared with individual pricing; combos are available under the Combos section on the app and website.

Cancellation Policy

Order Cancellation by the Customer is only allowed for Cash on Delivery (COD) orders and in selected stores. If you wish to cancel, you can do so within 60 seconds of placing the order without any charges.

Cancellations are applicable only for orders placed through the official Domino’s India app and not for those made through other platforms. Once the delivery executive reaches your location (first barrier point), cancellation will not be allowed.

Refund Policy

If a digitally paid order is cancelled due to the unavailability of items at the restaurant, the amount will be refunded to your original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. However, please note that Domino’s is not responsible for any delays from the bank’s end.

Customer Support

FAQ's

Can I use two coupon codes on the same Domino's order?

Only one coupon code is permitted per order at Domino's. This applies to all coupon types, including printed, mobile, and email codes. Combining two codes on a single transaction is not permitted, per Domino's official terms and conditions.

Does Domino's offer free delivery?

Free delivery is available on orders above ₹149 at select Domino's stores when ordering via the app, website, or mobile site. Below this threshold, delivery charges apply and vary by store. Check the checkout page for the delivery fee applicable to your address.

Are there app-exclusive offers at Domino's?

Yes. The Domino's app carries discounts not available on aggregators or the website, including personalised promo codes and late-night order deals. New users receive up to 50% off (maximum ₹100) on their first three app orders. Credit and debit card offers from HDFC, ICICI, and SBI also apply on app orders.

Are coupon codes available on Pizza Mania orders?

No. Coupons and promo codes are not applicable to the Pizza Mania range, per Domino's official terms and conditions. For budget orders, the Everyday Value section offers two regular pizzas from ₹99 each or two medium pizzas from ₹199 each, with no coupon required.

Does Domino's give 50% off on the first order?

Yes, new users can receive up to 50% off (maximum ₹100) on their first three orders placed via the official Domino's app. This offer does not apply to Everyday Value sections, Pizza Mania, or combo deals. Check the Offers section in the Domino's app for the currently active new-user code.

What bank and UPI payment offers are available on Domino's?

Domino's supports credit and debit card offers from HDFC, ICICI, SBI, and AMEX, along with UPI cashback via Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay, and Amazon Pay. Offer amounts change periodically. Check the Domino's Offers page or the payment screen at checkout for promotions currently applicable to your card or UPI app.
