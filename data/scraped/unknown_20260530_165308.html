About Meesho

Social media is as useful as it is fun. You can earn money by selling products over WhatsApp. Meesho not only lets you make money by selling their products online but also opens up ways to earn extra money every week as a bonus. Meesho is a company that started in 2015 by college graduates. Since then, it has had massive success in terms of products sold and customer satisfaction.

How does Meesho work?

Do you use Whatsapp or Facebook? That's all you need to earn with Meesho. When you become a part of the Meesho community by registering yourself into the portal, you will have access to hundreds of products across fashion, lifestyle, home decor, technology, and more. All you have to do is browse through the catalogs and find the products that the people in your circle might be interested in. You can share the products in your friends' groups or family contacts over WhatsApp.

How to place an order with Meesho?

Once you find someone who wants to buy the item, go ahead and place the order for them through Meesho's mobile app. At this time, you can see the product's price and the shipping cost. Here is where you add your commission. You can decide the commission yourself. Usually, the commission is between 20 to 60%, but this is up to you to decide.

Meesho Payment Policy

The cost of the order being places can be either done online or through Cash On Delivery. The online accepted modes of payment are credit and debit cards, net banking, wallets, PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, UPI. If you elect to pay online, you will pay the amount excluding the commission you added. While you collect the price of the order from the customer, you will collect the full amount, and that'show you will get your commission. If you are not comfortable with online payment on behalf of a customer, you can select COD as the payment method. Meesho's delivery partners will collect the payment, including the commission while delivering the parcel. Post confirmation, your commission will be transferred to your bank account. Transfers happen every 10 days in each month.

Return Policy at Meesho

If your customer has received the parcel but wants to return it, you can do so through the app within 7 days from the delivery. Go to the Orders section, select the order you would like to return, and select Returns. You will attach images of the defective part of the product and also fill in some other details. The product will be picked up from the customer, and the refund will be processed by Meesho.
