McDonald's FAQ's

What is the Rs. 99 meal at McDonald's?

The Rs. 99 Meal at McDonald’s is a budget-friendly combo that offers great taste at an affordable price. It includes a McAloo Tikki Burger, a serving of crispy fries, and a refreshing beverage.

How to use McDonald's coupon codes?

Download the McDelivery app, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Tap "Apply Coupon," enter your code, and the discount will be applied to your order instantly.

How to get a McDonald's Happy Pocket Card?

To get a Happy Pocket Card, you can do these few things: walk into a McDonald’s restaurant, inquire at the counter if they have the Happy Pocket card available, or you can also check out their website and find out the terms and conditions of the Happy Pocket card.

Is it okay to redeem multiple coupon codes from McDonald’s?

No, McDonald's only allows one deal or coupon code per person, per visit. Each offer must be redeemed in a separate transaction

Which is the best offer today at McDonald’s?

You get a Free McAloo Tikki + Fries + Veg Pizza McPuff offer on Orders Above Rs 349 today. Use code GB349 to avail of the offer. McDonald's shares free offers every day. You can check out their official deals page or GrabOn to know more about them.

How to get a discount on the McDonald’s app?

The thing is, offers are not automatically added to your orders in the app, so you need to redeem your offers via the “Rewards & Offers” section of the app.

Is there a student discount at McDonald’s?

While McDonald’s offers student discounts in countries like Malaysia, the UK, and Ireland, it does not offer a student discount in India. Students need to provide a valid student ID card at the restaurant to avail of the offer. However, the offer may change, so keep checking their official website for any updates.

Is there a minimum order value at McDonald’s?

No, there is no minimum order amount at McDonald’s. You can order just fries or even just a burger. However, delivery fees may apply if you are ordering it.

What’s the cheapest burger at McDonald’s?

The McAloo Tikki burger, a vegetarian option seems to be the cheapest burger available at McDonald’s India right now. It is priced at Rs.75 on their official website, but additional shipping costs may apply.

What is the free item you can get from McDonald’s on your birthday?

If you order from McDonald’s on your birthday you receive a free medium french fries. However, you need to be part of the rewards program to be able to access the offer.
