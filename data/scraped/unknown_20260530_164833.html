Myntra's Shipping Policy

Myntra delivers the product without any shipping fee if you shop with it for the first time. The company does not charge any shipping fee for orders above Rs. 1199 and charges a delivery fee of Rs. 149 for orders below Rs. 1199.

Myntra's Cancellation Policy

It has an easy cancellation policy. You can go to the ‘My Orders' section on the platform and select the order you want to cancel. An order can be canceled at any time if the cancel option is available on the website/app. Once the order is canceled, the company will initiate the refund process at the earliest.

Myntra's Return and Exchange Policy

Myntra operates a very convenient return and exchange policy. The company accepts returns and exchanges for almost all the products, provided they are in their original condition. However, the company takes only size exchanges. If you want to exchange the product for another product, you must first return the product and then buy another in its place. Below are the products that cannot be exchanged or returned once purchased -

Precious jewelry, socks, cosmetics, deodorants, RayBan sunglasses, shapewear bottoms, Swarovski, and perfumes.

It offers a free pick-up service in most locations. If there is no pick-up service available at your location, you can visit the store and get products yourself, and the company will later credit the shipping fee as a credit.

The company provides an instant refund for PhonePe Wallet users and Myntra credit users. On the other hand, online refunds are processed within 7-10 business days after refund initiation.

What is the Myntra Platform fee?

Myntra’s platform fee is a nominal charge that is added to orders to cover the cost of operations and continuous improvement of the platform. Myntra Platform fee typically ranges from Rs. 10 to Rs. 29. The fee applies to most users, including Myntra Insider members. It is usually non-refundable. You can try to save on platform fees by opting for digital payment methods like UPI, Credit/Debit Card, or Net Banking. Try to avoid placing COD orders.

Has Myntra stopped Supercoins?

Yes, as of February 2025, Myntra has stopped issuing Supercoins on purchases. However, as a Myntra Insider, you can get direct discounts and rewards on the 28th of every month.

How can I add money to my Myntra Credit?

  • Add a Myntra Gift Card to your Myntra Credit.

Can I place a return request for the Mnow order?

Yes, you can place a return request via the My Orders page on the Myntra app/website as long as the product is returnable and the return request is placed within the return window. If you have placed an Mnow order and want to cancel it, you can do so before the order is packed.
